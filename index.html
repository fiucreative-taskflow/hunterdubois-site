06 · Client WorkLove Court Visuals · Apr 2026

The Vertex Cup Miami.

A first-year U16 international youth tournament with the national sides of the USA, England, Mexico and Argentina, played April 13 to 23, 2026 at Beyond Bancard Field on FIU's campus. I was creative lead and social media manager for Vertex Soccer, starting from a brand new account.

Matchday 1 · Vertex Cup Miami
7.2MTotal views, Instagram and Facebook
404.6KAccounts reached
287.3KContent interactions
78.9KInstagram profile visits
The challenge

A new event with a new account and no player footage before the tournament. The launch had to make something that hadn't happened yet feel big and credible.

The approach

I built comprehensive social coverage from launch through the final whistle: hype videos, match highlights, photo galleries, player interviews, media day content, and daily recaps for all four national teams, with English and Spanish content for Argentina and Mexico. Before kickoff, with no player footage yet, I used an AI production pipeline to create a cinematic launch trailer that made the event feel established from day one.
